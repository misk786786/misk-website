Our Story

Founded by Abdurrahman Dhorat in Leicester in 2026, MISK Fragrances was created with a passion for fragrance and a desire to bring beautiful fragrance oils to everyone.

MISK focuses on creating an experience that combines quality, elegance and memorable scents. Every fragrance is chosen with care to help you discover something that feels special.

From rich oud and smooth musk to floral and fruity blends, MISK continues to explore different fragrance styles while keeping quality and customer experience at the heart of the brand.
