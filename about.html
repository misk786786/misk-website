Our Story

MISK Fragrances was founded by Abdurrahman Dhorat in Leicester in 2026, from a passion for fragrance and beautiful fragrance oils.

Our goal is simple: to create fragrances that combine quality, elegance and memorable scents while giving everyone the opportunity to discover something they truly enjoy.

From rich oud and smooth musk to floral and fruity blends, MISK brings together a variety of fragrance styles for different tastes and personalities.

Quality

We focus on creating premium fragrance oils with scents that are carefully selected and enjoyable to wear.

Elegance

Every MISK fragrance is designed to bring a sense of character, style and sophistication.

Your Experience

Our aim is to make discovering and enjoying fragrance a memorable experience from start to finish.
